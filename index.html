Shruti Bhimsaria

  • Incoming PhD Student, London School of Economics and Political Science

I am a climate and development economist, policy researcher, and executive leader working at the intersection of sustainable growth, climate policy, and development across emerging economies in the Global South. I am also an incoming PhD student at the London School of Economics. At LSE, I also direct research and policy for the Economics of Environment and Energy (EEE) Programme and head the Economics of Climate Organisation (ECO), a global research initiative bringing together economists, funders, and policymakers to build country-level programmes across Indonesia, India, Brazil, and Ethiopia. As Sustainability Advisor to the International Growth Centre (IGC), I support the organisation’s climate and sustainability strategy, lead the development of new country programmes, and provide strategic oversight to initiatives including the India Sustainable Growth Hub, the Energy Insights and Innovation Lab and a new Indonesia programme. My work spans climate finance, carbon markets, poverty, and the broader economics of climate and sustainable growth. Alongside research, I am very interested in building institutions, partnerships, and research ecosystems that strengthen the connection between academic evidence and public policy.

I am primarily based in London, UK, but spend as much time as I can in India, where my husband and family live, and where, conveniently, lot of my work happens too. It is a setup that keeps me perpetually torn between time zones, airports, and homes.
