I am an incoming PhD student at the London School of Economics. I work at the intersection of sustainable growth, climate policy, and development across emerging economies in the Global South, with a focus on climate finance, carbon markets, and poverty. At LSE, I direct research and policy for the Economics of Environment and Energy (EEE) Programme and head the Economics of Climate Organisation (ECO), a global research initiative convening economists, funders, and policymakers on country-level programmes across Indonesia, India, Brazil, and Ethiopia. As Sustainability Advisor to the International Growth Centre (IGC), I support the organisation's climate and sustainability strategy, lead the scoping of new country programmes, and provide strategic oversight to initiatives including the India Sustainable Growth Hub and the Energy Insights and Innovation Lab. Across these roles, I work to strengthen the links between academic research, policy engagement, and institutional partnerships.
