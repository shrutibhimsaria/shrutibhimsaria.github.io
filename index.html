Shruti Bhimsaria

Research and Policy Director, Economics of Environment and Energy (EEE) Programme — incoming PhD student, LSE

Abstract

I work at the intersection of climate and development economics, translating research on climate finance, carbon markets, and poverty into policy that governments and institutions can act on. I currently direct research and policy at the EEE Programme at LSE and head the Economics of Climate Organisation (ECO), a global research initiative convening economists, funders, and policymakers on climate finance and country-level programmes across Indonesia, India, Brazil, and Ethiopia. I also advise the International Growth Centre (IGC) on sustainability across South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Africa.
